# JunuBlink AI - Scholarship & Grant Finder

**JunuBlink AI** is a Streamlit-based web application that helps South Sudanese youth find legitimate scholarships and grants while protecting them from scams.

## Features

✅ **Smart Scholarship Matching** - Analyzes your profile and matches you with relevant scholarships and grants
✅ **Bilingual Support** - Available in English and Juba Arabic
✅ **Scam Protection** - Built-in warnings about common scholarship scams
✅ **Easy Application Guide** - Step-by-step instructions for safe application process
✅ **Downloadable Results** - Export your scholarship matches as a text file

## What You'll Find

- **African Leadership Scholarship** - Up to $15,000 for eligible students
- **Tech4Africa Innovation Grant** - $2,000-$10,000 for tech-focused students
- **UNHCR Education Grant** - Support for refugees and IDPs
- **Women in Leadership** - Special opportunities for female students
- **Disability & Inclusive Education** - Grants for students with disabilities

## How to Use

1. Fill in your personal information
2. Provide your academic background
3. Share your financial and social status
4. Click "Check My Eligibility"
5. View your matched opportunities
6. Follow the safe application guidelines
7. Download your results

## Safety First

⚠️ **Remember:** Legitimate scholarships are FREE. Never pay upfront fees.

### Common Scams to Avoid:
- Requests for money upfront
- Guaranteed scholarship promises
- Pressure to decide quickly
- Requests for sensitive bank information
- No clear organization details
